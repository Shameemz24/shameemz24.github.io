Delete your data

UK49s Results (Android app com.shameemz.uk49s) by ShamzUK49

UK49s Results has no user accounts. Most data stays on your phone, and the small amount stored on our server exists only while you use match alerts. You can remove it yourself at any time.

1. Delete it yourself in the app

  1. My Numbers match alerts: open the My Numbers tab, tap Edit on a line and choose Delete this line, or switch its Alert off. When no alert lines are left, your saved numbers and notification token are removed from our server straight away.
  2. Results alerts: turn off Notify me when results are in on the Results tab.
  3. Everything on your phone: uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → UK49s Results → Storage → Clear storage.

2. Or ask us to delete it

Email shameem.hoosain@gmail.com with the subject "UK49s Results data deletion". We delete any server-side data linked to your device within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your saved My Numbers lines and alert settings
  • The push-notification token used to send you alerts
  • The anonymous ID the app uses to keep your alert settings private

What we keep

  • Premium purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as required for billing, refunds and tax. Your Premium unlock stays linked to your Google account so you can restore it.
  • Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
