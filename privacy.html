Privacy Policy

UK49s Results (Android app com.shakeelw.uk49s) · Last updated 28 September 2026

UK49s Results is an unofficial app that shows UK49s draw results and statistics. It is not affiliated with UK49s, Lottery Ltd, or any lottery operator, and you cannot buy tickets in it. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

1. No account needed

You don't sign up or give us your name, email, or phone number to use the app.

2. Data stored on your phone

  • Your saved numbers ("My Numbers"), theme, and settings are stored on your device.
  • If you turn on match alerts (Premium), your saved numbers, the draws you chose, and a push-notification token are sent to our server (Google Firebase) so we can tell you when your numbers match. Turning alerts off or deleting a line removes it from the server.

3. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob shows ads to free users. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, and basic device information to show and measure ads. In the UK and EEA we ask for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. See how Google uses data for ads.
  • Google Play Billing and RevenueCat process the one-time Premium purchase. We receive an anonymous app user ID and your purchase status, never your card details. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications using a device token. See the Firebase privacy information.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or files.
  • We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. The app is for adults only.

5. Security and retention

All data sent from the app is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Alert data is kept only while alerts are switched on. Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your phone.

6. Your choices and deletion requests

You can change ad consent in the app's settings, turn off notifications in Android settings, and delete saved lines in the app. To ask us to delete any server-side data, email us at the address below.
